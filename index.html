Retail Solar: Out of Reach

A professionally installed solar system costs $15,000–$30,000 after equipment, labour, and permits. Payback periods of 8–12 years make it impractical for renters, those planning to move, or households without capital.

Rates Only Go One Direction

The U.S. Energy Information Administration confirms residential electricity rates have increased in nearly every year for the past two decades. Staying fully grid-dependent means absorbing every rate increase indefinitely.

What a DIY Blueprint Changes

A proper engineering blueprint eliminates contractor labour — the largest cost in any energy project. When you have exact specifications, a clear parts list, and wiring diagrams, the barrier becomes time, not money.

On-Demand vs. Passive Generation
